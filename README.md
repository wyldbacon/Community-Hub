# Community Hub
> **Open-source, community-owned digital infrastructure for local action.**

Community Hub is an independent social platform built for real-world communities across the UK and beyond. It gives neighbours, local organisers, and grassroots collectives an autonomous digital space to coordinate action, share resources, and pool funds — completely free from commercial ads, tracking, or corporate algorithms.

---

## 🏡 The Big Idea: Reclaiming the Digital Commons

Mainstream social platforms were built to extract attention, data, and profit from our towns and cities. When a local community group, mutual aid network, or campaign uses corporate social media, their hard work generates value for overseas shareholders — while their reach is choked by algorithms.

Community Hub turns this on its head:

* **Zero Central Ownership:** Built like a digital commons. The platform belongs to the communities and organisers who use it, not corporate investors.
* **Keeping Value Local:** Any economic value generated stays within the ecosystem. Instead of enriching platform bosses, value cycles back into a shared pot to fund local action.
* **Built for Real-World Impact:** Designed to strengthen real-world social fabric, from neighbourhood projects and parish halls to national grassroots movements.

---

## 🎯 What We're Fixing

### 1. Corporate Algorithms vs Local Autonomy
* **The Problem:** Commercial platforms profit by keeping people hooked and divided, treating local social fabric like raw data to mine.
* **Our Response:**
  * **Chronological Feeds:** Clean, distraction-free feeds with zero ad-tracking or algorithmic manipulation.
  * **Data Sovereignty:** You own your profile and connections. Groups set their own rules without arbitrary censorship.
  * **Open Governance:** Platform decisions and updates are transparent, open-source, and auditable.

### 2. Fragile Tools for Local Organisers
* **The Problem:** Local groups are forced to juggle fragmented tools — WhatsApp chats, Facebook pages, and spreadsheets — that can lock them out or hide their posts overnight.
* **Our Response:**
  * **All-in-One Local Space:** Groups, events, resource registers, and mutual aid funding in one straightforward app.
  * **Resilient Tech:** Independent, open-source software that no corporation can buy out, shut down, or monetise by stealth.
  * **Privacy First:** Strong safety defaults to protect organisers, volunteers, and vulnerable members.

### 3. Wealth Extraction vs Community Wealth Building
* **The Problem:** When neighbours create content, host events, and build networks online, commercial platforms capture 100% of the financial value.
* **Our Response:**
  * **Co-operative Spirit:** Ownership of Community Hub is shared across active members and local groups.
  * **Equitable Exchange:** If the platform generates revenue (through ethical data partnerships or premium services), a built-in community return mechanism cycles funds straight back to active groups and members.
